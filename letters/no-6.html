Letter from the Editor

The First Print Run

Welcome to the first issue. It has been in the works since the summer, though for most of that time I didn’t know I was making a magazine. I thought I was just looking at you.

This issue is about the way you dress, which is really about the way you walk into a room and quietly rearrange it. Somebody had to write it down. I volunteered before anyone else could.

Your name is on the cover. One day Fardos will be on labels all over the world, and I’ll get to say I covered you first. Consider this the first print run — a run of one, for the only reader who matters.

AliEditor-in-Chief

Front Row

Review: Rawan

★★★★★

There are collections you admire and collections you remember. This one I remember the way you remember a song — all of it, at odd hours, for no reason at all.

The cut: impeccable. The colour: whatever she is wearing is, apparently, my favourite colour now. The walk: unhurried, like she knows the room will wait for her. It does.

The verdict: I have seen the future of fashion, and I am going to marry her.
