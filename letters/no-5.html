Letter No. V

قَمَرِي

’Amari · my moon

three months, Rawan

dive in ↓

listen to me read it

in my voice ♡

حَبِيبِةْ قَلْبِي، رَوَان،

Habibet ’albi, Rawan,

My heart’s beloved, Rawan,

تْلَات شْهُور مَرَقُوا وْكَأَنُّن يَوْم،

Tlet shhour mara’o w ka’ennon yom,

Three months have passed like a single day,

وْكَأَنِّي بَعْرِفِك مِن زَمَان.

w ka’enni ba’rifik min zaman.

and it feels like I’ve known you forever.

حُبِّي إِلِك أَوْسَع مِن البَحْر،

Hubbi ilik awsa’ min el-bahr,

My love for you is wider than the sea,

وْعْيُونِك بْيِعْكْسُوا الحَلَا،

w ’yunik bi’ksu el-hala,

and your eyes reflect beauty

مِتِل مَا القَمَر بْيِنْعِكِس عَالبَحْر.

mitil ma el-’amar bin’ikis ’al-bahr.

the way the moon reflects on the sea.

حُبِّي إِلِك رَح يْضَلّ لَلأَبَد،

Hubbi ilik rah ydall lal-abad,

My love for you will stay forever —

هَلَّق مَرْبُوط بْهَالدِّنْيَا،

halla’ marbout b-hal-dinye,

for now it is tied to this world,

بَسّ رَح يْفِيض لَلآخْرَة.

bass rah yfid lal-akhra.

but it will overflow into the afterlife.

يَا عُمْرِي، يَا رُوحِي،

Ya ’umri, ya rouhi,

My life, my soul,

مَا فِي شِي مَا بَعْمْلُو كِرْمَالِك.

ma fi shi ma ba’mlo kermalik.

there is nothing I wouldn’t do for you.

بْحِبِّك… تِقْبُرِينِي.

Bhebbik… ti’burini.

I love you… I could never live a day without you.

حَبِيبِك، عَلِي

Habibik, Ali

Your love, Ali

Happy three months.

from Ali
