Letter No. V

قَمَرِي

’Amari · my moon

three months, Rawan

dive in ↓

listen to me read it

in my voice ♡

رَوَان، يَا قَمَرِي،

Rawan, ya ’amari,

Rawan, my moon,

إِنْتِي أَحْلَى شِي بْنَظَرِي.

inti ahla shi b-nazari.

you’re the most beautiful thing in my eyes.

مِتِل القَمَر عَالبَحْر،

Mitil el-’amar ’al-bahr,

Like the moon on the sea,

إِنْتِي بِتْنَوّْرِي عُمْرِي.

inti bitnawwri ’umri.

you light up my life.

إِنْتِي إِلِي،

Inti ili,

You are mine,

بِهَالدِّنْيَا وْبِالآخْرَة.

bi-haddinye w bil-akhra.

in this life and the next.

يَا رُوحِي،

Ya rouhi,

My soul,

قَلْبِي مَعِك وَيْن مَا تْرُوحِي.

’albi ma’ik wen ma trouhi.

my heart is with you wherever you go.

بْحِبِّك… تِقْبُرِينِي.

Bhebbik… ti’burini.

I love you… I could never live a day without you.

حَبِيبِك، عَلِي

Habibik, Ali

Your love, Ali

Happy three months.

from Ali
