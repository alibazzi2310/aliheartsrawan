Flowers for You

for Rawan

My dearest Rawan,

I chose them one stem at a time,
each for something I couldn’t say.

Not the kind wrapped up in a shop —
these I cut, and tied, and got wrong twice.

what each stem means

Ranunculus
I am dazzled by you
Peony
a happy life together
Lavender
devotion
Gypsophila
everlasting love
Eucalyptus
protection

They’ll wilt by Sunday, I know.
I’ll bring you more — that’s rather the point,
Rawan.

care instructions

Trim the stems every few days.
Change the water on Sundays.
Keep them where the light is best —
they were picked by someone who
thinks about you constantly.

I love you.

from Ali
