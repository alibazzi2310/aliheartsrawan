For Rawan

a little letter, just for you

open me ↓

My dearest Rawan,

In quiet mornings, soft and slow,
your name is the first thing I know.
Like lavender light on a windowsill,
you steady my heart, and always will.

✿ ✿ ✿

If I could paint the way you laugh,
I'd need the sky, and then its half.
Every shade of pink and mauve
still wouldn't hold the way I love.

✿ ✿ ✿

So here's a page, a tiny start,
a folded corner of my heart.
Wherever you go, whatever you do —
it beats, quite simply, because of you.

I adore you.

from Ali
